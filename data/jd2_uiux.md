# JD2: Growth For Impact UI/UX Internship

Source: https://tally.so/r/0QovpQ (full text)

growthforimpact.co: building "LinkedIn for sustainability".

## Role overview
We're seeking a highly creative and detail-oriented UX/UI Designer Intern to join Growth For Impact. As the first member of our design team, you will play a crucial role in shaping the user experience and visual design of our digital products, including our website and job board.

## Key responsibilities
- Design and develop engaging user interfaces for our website and job board, ensuring a seamless user experience across various devices and platforms.
- Conduct user research to understand user psychology and behavior, informing design decisions that meet user needs.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to develop and implement design solutions that align with business objectives.
- Create high-fidelity prototypes and design specifications using Figma.
- Participate in design critiques and feedback sessions to ensure design quality and consistency.
- Stay up to date with industry trends and best practices in UX/UI design.
- Maintain a cohesive design language and visual identity.

## Expected qualifications
**Experience:** open to candidates with 0 to 1 year of experience in UX/UI design. Recent graduates and people transitioning into design are encouraged to apply.

**Skills and tools**
- Proficiency in UI/UX design principles and human-centered design methodologies.
- Experience with Figma and prototyping tools.
- Strong understanding of user psychology and behavior.
- Excellent aesthetic sense and attention to detail.

## Mandatory requirements
- Must be great at understanding user psychology.
- Creative outlook, without fear of experimentation.
- An eye for detail.
- Great aesthetic sense.

## What makes this role special
First person on the design team; work on projects with real impact in the sustainability industry. Fully remote, work from anywhere.

## What success looks like after 90 days
- Helping to revamp the entire website for a seamless user experience and visually appealing design.
- Building the best possible solution for the job board, incorporating user feedback and testing results.

**Stipend:** 10,000 to 15,000 per month.

Note: aesthetic sense, creative outlook and eye for detail cannot be measured from resume text. The prototype does not score them; the reviewer should judge them from the candidate's portfolio or projects.
