# JD1: LCA Consultant (Chennai based / relocate)

Source: https://tally.so/r/9q5plp (full text)

## About the organization
Our client is a leading global sustainability consultancy based in Pune, serving the manufacturing, construction, healthcare, and corporate sectors.

**Location:** Chennai (onsite)

## Requirements
Their client needs a dedicated LCA consultant to support sustainability goals by building Life Cycle Inventory (LCI) datasets, running LCA studies, and comparing results against industry peers.

The candidate needs to relocate to Chennai for this role. The company can help with relocation.

## Key responsibilities
- **Data collection:** Consultant must collect and compile Life Cycle Inventory (LCI) data.
- **Data validation and quality control:** Consultant must validate and standardize all LCI data for accuracy, completeness, and consistency. Any missing data must be documented thoroughly.
- **LCA modelling:** Consultant must build the LCI model using life cycle inventory data collected in the software.
- **Assumptions:** All assumptions made during LCA modelling must be clearly documented to ensure datasets and models are fully traceable and audit-ready.
- **Operational data:** Consultant will collaborate with the client's cross-functional teams (CFTs) to gather operational data. This includes plant level assembly details, resources, energy use, and complete logistics data (inbound/outbound transportation modes, distances and supplier logistics).
- **Public benchmarking:** Consultant must collect and compile relevant benchmarking data from public sources, including databases, industry reports, research papers, academic studies, regulatory filings, and previous LCA research.
- **Duties:** Consultant must adhere to all project deadlines and deliver high-quality work.

## Requirements
- **Experience:** 2 to 3 years of experience running Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) projects independently.
- **Software expertise:** Hands-on experience using either GaBi or SimaPro.
- **Project history:** Completed at least 3 LCA projects.
- **Standards:** Strictly follow ISO 14040 and ISO 14044 guidelines for all project work.
- **Onsite location:** Must work onsite at the client location, closely with their sustainability team.
- **Office rules:** Follow all client workplace rules, including working full-time from the office, following work hours, and tracking attendance.
- **Stakeholder management:** Represent our client and maintain long-term relationships with customers.

## Candidate profile
- **Education:** Bachelor's or Master's (environmental domain highly desired).
- **Language:** Fluent spoken and written English.
- **Skills:** Experience in Life Cycle Assessment studies.
- **Attributes:** Flexible, self-motivating, and able to manage multiple tasks.
- **Travel:** High willingness to travel extensively across India.
- **Commitment:** Minimum commitment of 1 year.

## Preferred qualifications
- Prior experience in LCA and Environmental Product Declaration (EPD).
- Basic understanding of business finance and accounting.
- Proactive initiative to learn and ideate new solutions in a dynamic team.

## Compensation
- Salary: as per industry level (finalized based on qualifications and skills).
- Location: onsite at client location.
